# Chryso-Chok Product — (v0.2)

An app that helps students and early-career professionals handle difficult conversations with greater empathy and confidence - via short lessons, role-play practice, and carefully bounded real-life guidiance.
## Target Users

-Primary: Students and early-career professionals who want practical help with misunderstandings, disagreements, and emotionally difficult conversations.
-Secondary: Educators, team leaders, and community organizers who want to strengthen empathy, active listening and clear communication.

## Problem

Even with the best intentions, people can struggle to respond fairly when emotions run high, stress builds, or perspectives clash. Without easy-to-use tools for self-awareness and structured practice, these moments often escalate rather than resolve.
