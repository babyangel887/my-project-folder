Short Lesson: Pause before reacting

1. Question: Have you ever said something in the heat of the moment that you regretted?

2. Idea: Pause + breathe = space to choose words.

3. Example: Before: "That's wrong!" After: "Can you help me understand what you meant?"

Real Situation Guidance — 3-part shape

You: My teammate keeps interrupting me in meetings.

1. Reflect back: It sounds like your teammate keeps interrupting you during meetings. (I'm assuming coworker, not manager — let me know if wrong.)

2. Options:
• A direct option: "Can I finish my point, then I want your take?"
• A softer option: "I get excited too — can we take turns?"
• A question-based option: "Can you help me understand what you meant?"

3. Reminder: You know this situation best; take what's useful and leave the rest.
